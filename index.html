What I do

Hazard analysis and specification

SIL determination
LOPA studies establishing the integrity target for each safety function, traced to the hazard it protects against.
Safety requirements specification
Requirements written so they can actually be verified, and traced back to the hazard analysis that demanded them.

Design and realisation

Safety instrumented system design
SIS design lead: safety philosophy, architecture, basis of design, functional and detailed design specifications, software and V&V plans, device selection and proof-test regimes, on projects where I am engaged as designer rather than assessor.
SIL verification
Probability of failure on demand, architectural constraints and systematic capability: the three requirements a claimed SIL has to meet, with the assumptions laid out to be audited.
Application programming review
Safety application software reviewed against IEC 61511 clause 12.
HIMA safety PLCs: SILworX and ELOP II (certified engineer, 2014–2020); FSAs on PLC-based SISs since
Siemens S7 (Step 7): transferable to S7 F through common IEC 61508 principles

Verification, validation and assessment

Design verification and substantiation
Substantiation reports on existing and proposed designs, including legacy systems with thin documentation.
Validation witnessing
Factory and site acceptance testing witnessed on the client's behalf, against the safety requirements rather than the vendor's test script.
Independent functional safety assessment
FSA stages 1 to 5, as lead assessor or independent checker, carried out independently of the design and delivery organisation.
Independent checking
Verification of FSA reports and other safety deliverables prepared by other practitioners or assessors.

Management and competence

Functional safety management systems
Writing the procedures the standard expects (safety lifecycle, competency management, management of change, verification and audit), or gap-assessing the ones already in place.
Client-side safety authority
Specifying and assuring a vendor's safety system work, and holding functional safety management across a contract.
IEC 61508 and 61511 training
Lifecycle training delivered to engineering and operations teams, built around the systems in the room rather than generic worked examples.
