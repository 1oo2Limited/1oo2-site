What I do

Independent functional safety assessment
FSA stages 1 to 5, carried out independently of the design and delivery organisation.
SIL determination and verification
Target integrity levels set and proven, with the calculations and assumptions laid out to be audited.
Safety requirements specification
Requirements written so they can actually be verified, and traced back to the hazard analysis.
Design verification and substantiation
Substantiation reports on existing and proposed designs, including legacy systems with thin documentation.
Application programming review
Safety application software reviewed against IEC 61511 clause 12.
Client-side safety authority
Specifying and assuring a vendor's safety system work, and holding functional safety management across a contract.
Functional safety management systems
Writing the procedures the standard expects — safety lifecycle, competency management, management of change, verification and audit — or gap-assessing the ones already in place.
Safety instrumented system design
SIS architecture, device selection, SIL verification calculations and proof-test regimes, on projects where I am engaged as designer rather than assessor.
